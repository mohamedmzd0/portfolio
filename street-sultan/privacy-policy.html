Street Sultan · سلطان الشوارعPrivacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · App: Street Sultan (package com.mohamedmzd.streetsultan) · Developer: Mohamed (MohamedMZD)

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Street Sultan (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. The App is a street racing game for Android. We do not require an account and we do not ask you for your name, email, phone number or any other personal details.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress only on your device: in-game cash, selected car, paint colour, driver and language. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: full-screen ads between races and optional rewarded ads (for example “watch an ad to double your cash” or “watch an ad to continue”). To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interactions (ads shown, clicks, rewarded ads completed) and diagnostic data.

Google uses this data as described in its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads). If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, ads are shown according to the consent choices required by law in your region.

4. Driver voice (text-to-speech)

During races your driver speaks short lines (for example “Let’s go!”). These are produced by the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone. Only fixed game phrases are spoken; the App does not use your microphone and does not record or send any audio.

5. Game engine and platform

The App is built with the Unity engine and runs on Google Play services. These may process limited technical data (such as crash and performance information) needed for the App to work. See the Unity Game Player Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions

The App uses the Internet permission (to load ads) and the advertising ID permission (used by AdMob). It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise GPS location.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data sharing, retention and security

We do not sell personal information and we do not receive or store personal data on our own servers. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept and protected according to Google’s policies. Game progress stays on your device until you delete the App or its data.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best made through your device’s ad settings or to Google. You can always contact us for help.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: mohamedmzd0@gmail.com

3. الإعلانات (Google AdMob)

التطبيق مجاني ويعرض إعلانات عبر Google AdMob التابعة لشركة Google LLC: إعلانات بملء الشاشة بين السباقات، وإعلانات اختيارية بمكافأة (مثل «شاهد إعلانًا لمضاعفة المكسب» أو «شاهد إعلانًا لإكمال السباق»). لعرض الإعلانات وقياس أدائها قد تجمع Google وتعالج:

  • المعرّف الإعلاني لجهازك (Android Advertising ID)؛
  • معلومات الجهاز والتطبيق (الطراز، نظام التشغيل، اللغة، إصدار التطبيق)؛
  • عنوان IP والموقع التقريبي المستنتج منه؛
  • التفاعل مع الإعلانات (الإعلانات المعروضة، النقرات، إكمال الإعلانات بمكافأة) وبيانات تشخيصية.

تستخدم Google هذه البيانات وفق سياساتها: سياسة خصوصية Google وكيف تستخدم Google المعلومات من المواقع والتطبيقات التي تستخدم خدماتها.

اختياراتك: يمكنك إعادة تعيين المعرّف الإعلاني أو حذفه، أو إيقاف الإعلانات المخصّصة، من إعدادات هاتفك (الإعدادات ← Google ← الإعلانات أو الإعدادات ← الخصوصية ← الإعلانات). إذا كنت في المنطقة الاقتصادية الأوروبية أو المملكة المتحدة أو سويسرا، تُعرض الإعلانات وفق خيارات الموافقة التي يفرضها القانون في منطقتك.
